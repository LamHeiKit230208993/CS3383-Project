# User Needs: Budget-Conscious Shopper

[Request source and shared terms](../product-description.md) · [Team decisions](../project-scope.md)

## Situation

The shopper has a fixed household budget and limited storage space. They compare regular goods on a phone before shopping. They want to save money without spending an evening checking several store apps.

## Requests

- “Show the same brand and pack across stores together, so I do not compare the wrong item.”
- “Let me compare the cost of my whole list, not just one cheap item.”
- “Show the cost per gram or litre when pack sizes differ.”
- “Tell me about delivery costs and stock before I choose a store.”
- “Let me track my usual rice, oil, and detergent. Notify me only when a price meets my target or the change matters to me.”
- “Let me set quiet hours and pause alerts when I have enough at home.”
- “Show past prices and the date of the data so I can judge the offer.”
- “Help me plan weekly spending and see how it affects my monthly budget.”
- “Do not encourage a bulk purchase that I cannot store or use.”

## Problems to handle

- A 2 kg pack has a lower checkout price than a 5 kg pack. The shopper must still see that they are different products.
- A list has one item with no price. An incomplete total must not look like a complete cheaper basket.
- An offer requires two packs. The displayed cost must make the required purchase quantity clear.
- The same price arrives in several updates. Repeated alerts can cause the shopper to disable notifications.
- A saved shopping list contains planned purchases, not proof of money already spent.
